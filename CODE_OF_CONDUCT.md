# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we are building tools for people who spend their working lives trying to move money toward public good, often with too few resources and too little sleep. Treat contributors, users, and the people asking beginner questions the way you would want to be treated on your worst week. Assume good faith. Explain rather than dismiss.

Unacceptable behavior can be reported to **conduct@egeriacorp.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainers will respond within five business days.
